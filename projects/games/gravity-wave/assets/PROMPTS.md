# Gravity Wave source-art prompts

The three source atlases were generated in built-in ImageGen mode, then copied
unchanged into `assets/source/`. The deterministic local converter creates the
smaller Dreamcast-ready derivatives in `assets/generated/`.

## Terrain material atlas

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: Dreamcast game terrain material
> atlas, source art for four tileable low-resolution textures. Create one
> perfectly square 2-by-2 atlas with four equally sized square material
> quadrants: top-left weathered blue-black canyon basalt with subtle cyan
> mineral seams; top-right ancient jade-green mossy rock with luminous lime
> crystal flecks; bottom-left dark violet alien stone with magenta crystalline
> veins; bottom-right scorched ember-red sandstone with thin molten-gold
> cracks. Premium late-1990s / early-2000s console game texture art,
> hand-painted realistic stylization, rich material depth that remains readable
> after downsampling to 128x128 RGB565. Exact orthographic top-down surface
> textures; four equal quadrants separated only by a razor-thin neutral guide
> line; no perspective, objects, or outer border. Diffuse neutral
> material-reference lighting with baked crevice shading and no cast shadows.
> Multi-scale rock grain, erosion, cracks, and mineral deposits; each quadrant
> individually seamless at its own edges with even texel density. No text,
> labels, symbols, logos, watermark, scene elements, or large unique focal mark.
> Avoid photographs, obvious repetition, noisy microdetail, beveled UI panels,
> and metallic sci-fi panels.

## Vehicle and structure material atlas

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: Dreamcast game vehicle and structure
> material atlas, source art for four low-resolution model textures. Create one
> perfectly square 2-by-2 atlas with four equally sized square material
> quadrants: top-left sleek pearl-silver and midnight-blue aerospace hull
> plating with fine cyan panel seams and tiny rivets; top-right angular charcoal
> and crimson hostile armor plating with restrained orange emissive vents;
> bottom-left deep cyan-blue glass canopy material with layered reflections,
> hexagonal energy details, and subtle star highlights; bottom-right ivory and
> dark-gold ancient alien machinery panels with engraved geometric channels and
> turquoise energy inlays. Premium late-1990s / early-2000s console
> science-fiction texture art, hand-painted realistic stylization, highly
> readable after downsampling to 128x128 RGB565. Exact orthographic surface
> textures with four equal quadrants and only a razor-thin neutral guide line;
> no perspective, complete objects, or outer border. Diffuse neutral lighting
> with baked edge highlights and crevice shading. Painted metal, scratches,
> restrained wear, panel breaks, vents, and energy channels; individually
> seamless quadrants and even texel density. No text, letters, numbers, logos,
> watermark, scene elements, complete spacecraft silhouette, or large unique
> focal mark. Avoid modern ultra-clean UI, photos, excessive noise, weapon
> imagery, and real-brand-like symbols.

## Transparent biome-effects atlas

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent Dreamcast game billboard
> sprite atlas. Create one perfectly square 2-by-2 sprite atlas on a genuinely
> transparent background, with exactly one centered isolated sprite per equal
> quadrant: top-left a dense stylized alien jungle leaf-and-fern canopy cluster
> in deep emerald and luminous lime; top-right a soft pale-blue waterfall mist
> and cloud puff; bottom-left a violet-cyan crystalline energy wisp with
> branching arcs; bottom-right a compact orange-red flame and dark smoke plume.
> Premium late-1990s / early-2000s console game sprite art, hand-painted
> realistic stylization, strong readable silhouettes after downsampling to
> 64x64 ARGB4444. Exact 2-by-2 equal quadrants, each sprite centered with
> generous transparent padding and fully contained in its quadrant; no guide
> lines, background, or overlap. Emissive highlights and soft edge falloff for
> alpha blending. Genuinely transparent RGBA background; exactly four isolated
> sprites; no text, labels, logos, watermark, ground plane, scene, or rectangular
> backplates; clean alpha edges without white halos. Avoid a pixel-art grid,
> modern UI icons, complete objects, borders, and an opaque black background.
