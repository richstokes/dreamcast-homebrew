Get started on your Dreamcast

You’ll need a Dreamcast, a controller, a VMU and a broadband adapter connected by Ethernet to your router. The stock dial-up modem is not supported by the client.

1. Create your account

Register here with a username, password and email address. You’ll use the same login in the Dreamcast app.

2. Boot the app

Use the CDI image downloaded above to start the app on your console.

GDEMU: add the CDI to your SD card using your usual game setup, then launch it from the menu. For other optical drive replacements, check which image formats they support.

CD-R: burn the CDI as a disc image, rather than copying the file onto a data disc. It is self-booting on MIL-CD-compatible Dreamcasts; no separate boot disc is needed. Some later consoles cannot boot these discs.

GDEMU setup · Disc-burning guidance

3. Connect and log in

Connect the broadband adapter before starting. Your router should provide an address automatically through DHCP. Set the Dreamcast’s date and time correctly so secure connections can be verified.

Log in with your DCVMU account.

4. Upload or download a save

Upload: select Upload from the main menu, choose a save and switch VMUs with left/right if needed. Add a name, game and optional notes. Saves are public by default; choose private to keep one in your account. If a duplicate exists, you can replace it or keep another copy.

Download: select Download, browse your own saves or public saves, then choose a destination VMU with enough free blocks. Confirm the install or replacement. Keep the VMU connected and the console powered on until it finishes.

Follow the on-screen button prompts to select items and go back. A selects and B goes back.
